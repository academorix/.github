#### What an academy gets

- 🗓️ **A schedule that holds.** Sessions, venues and coaches in one calendar, with clashes caught before they happen.
- 📝 **Enrolment and billing.** Trials, registration, fees and reminders, without spreadsheets.
- 🏃 **Coaching.** Session plans, attendance and each athlete's progress.
- 🛟 **Safeguarding.** Who may collect a child, medical notes for the coach on duty, and a record of who saw what.
- 📱 **One app** for coaches and families, in the academy's own colours and language.
- 🌐 **The academy's website**, with booking built in.
